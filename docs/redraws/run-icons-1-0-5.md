# Redraws for the first consumer (1.0.5)

The first consumer of the package, a running app, reviewed every icon it shows at its own sizes and asked for these three redraws. Each was judged on a sheet at the catalogue size, 24 px at 1x.

- `running-shoe`: read as a clog; now a running shoe in profile with a collar, a tongue and two lace ticks (approval pending: the auditor, for the founder)
- `crown`: the points were low and the sides closed; now taller points with the outer ones opening outwards, a sixteen-pixel master with its own shape, and a filled variant that follows the outline (approval pending: the auditor, for the founder)
- `link`: a capsule with an inner stroke that read as a paperclip; now two open chain links on the diagonal, kept apart by the cut (approval pending: the auditor, for the founder)
