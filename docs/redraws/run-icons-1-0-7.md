# Redraws for the first consumer (1.0.7): the figures and the link family

At 192 px the figures had a back arm that hooked down, a torso that was a stub, a swimmer that read as a line chart, and a yoga figure that read as a T. The link read as one capsule split in two, and its five variants still carried the capsule of before 1.0.5. Each drawing below was redrawn from scratch on one construction, with no third-party set open: one spine, the head set off by the cut, both arms from the same shoulder point and both legs from the same hip point.

- `run`: a lean forward, the forward arm bent up, the back arm swinging back and down, the lead knee lifted and the back leg extended; a 16 px master was added (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `walk`: an upright spine, arms and legs swinging from one shoulder and one hip in a short stride (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link`: two interlocking oval links on the diagonal, the strand cut by the gap where one passes through the other (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link-off`: the new link with the slash, its strands stopping short of it (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link-plus`: the new link with the plus (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link-minus`: the new link with the minus (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link-check`: the new link with the check (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `link-x`: the new link with the cross (approved by the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `treadmill`: the runner rebuilt on the same construction, on the belt (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `airplane-landing`: a side-view airliner, nose 15 degrees below the horizontal, above the ground line; the top view pitched down read as a plane crashing (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `wheelchair`: the body, thigh, shin and one arm from the shared points, and a wheel open where the body is (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `yoga`: a warrior pose in place of a T with a triangle (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `swimming`: a front-crawl swimmer over the wave, in place of a line with a dot (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)
- `nurse`: the cap set off from the head by the cut, with its cross (approved by the auditor for the founder, 2026-11-21)

`accessibility` was checked against the same construction and left as it is: its arms leave the top of the spine, its legs leave the hip, and the head is set off by the cut.
