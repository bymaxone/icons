The third wave of B6-B, from the concepts open sets name. [`b6b3-1.png`](./b6b3-1.png) and [`b6b3-2.png`](./b6b3-2.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **`tree`** is the conifer, and **`tornado`** a stack of curved lines, each offset from the one above.
- **`scooter`** is the kick scooter, the micromobility one.
- **Currencies.** `currency-dollar`, `currency-euro`, `currency-pound`, `currency-rupee` and `currency-real` draw the currency sign, a typographic convention (spec section 3, item 5).
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.
