# B6-C1: interface, arrows and families

The first wave of B6-C, from the concepts five open sets name. [`b6c1-1.png`](./b6c1-1.png) to [`b6c1-3.png`](./b6c1-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Families keep their base.** The bookmark, clipboard, file, folder, database, shield, mail, phone and user variants draw the body of their family, with an open marker kept inside it or a closed one as a corner badge (spec section 2.5).
- **Corner arrows.** The eight `corner-*` icons are one arrow turned and mirrored: a straight shaft, a rounded bend and a chevron head.
- **`eye-closed`** is the closed lid with lashes, labelled Closed eye; `eye-off` keeps its slash and the label Hide.
- **At 16 px.** Rendered at 16 px and 1x and tested as in [`b2/sixteen-px.md`](../b2/sixteen-px.md), `file-download`, `file-image` and `file-x` fuse their crease with the outline, as every icon of the file family does at the family's cut, and `window` fuses its title bar with its frame. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`cursor-text`, `border-all`, `border-inner`, `alert-octagon`, `heading`, `apps` and the eight `corner-*` arrows are flagged and accepted as primitives or conventions (spec section 3, item 5): each is the shape the concept is, drawn on this grid and stroke.
