# B5d: commerce, communication, places and devices

The fourth sub-batch of B5. [`b5d-1.png`](./b5d-1.png) to [`b5d-4.png`](./b5d-4.png) show the 35 line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred; [`b5d-filled.png`](./b5d-filled.png) shows the four filled drawings beside their line drawings. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **The `-off` drawings** (`bell-off`, `cloud-off`, `video-off`, `camera-off`, `wifi-off`) start from the whole drawing and trim it where it comes within the cut of the set's bar, keeping the original curves. `camera-off` moves its hump right and keeps its lens as a crescent, since without them it reads as a crossed-out folder.
- **`phone-hang-up`** is the handset in the hang-up position, with no bar. The middle of a handset is its grip, so any bar across it hides the phone; `phone-off` is its alias.
- **`coins`** draws two flat coins, the front one hiding the back one with the cut between them. Two rings read as overlapping circles, and a stack of three reads as `database`.
- **`cart` and `bag`** stay apart: the cart in profile with a handle and two wheels, the bag from the front with arched handles. `bank` has no letter and `coins` no digit.
- **`smartphone`, `tablet` and `laptop`** are one family, with the camera at the top of each screen.
- **`smile`** opens its ring by the cut and closes its eyes in a laugh.
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.

## Similarity

`percent` (d = 0.01) is a primitive. The others under 0.30 are universal conventions: the `@` glyph (`at-sign`), the wireless symbol (`wifi`), the envelope (`mail`, as in `mail-invite`), the isometric box (`package`; a box seen from the front is `archive-box`), the folded map (`map`), the device outline (`smartphone`, `tablet`), the tray (`inbox`), the drop marker (`map-pin`) and the price tag (`tag`). `compass` splits its ring at the cardinal points, which took it from 0.20 to 0.38.
