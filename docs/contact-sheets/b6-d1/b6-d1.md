# B6-D1: family variants and state icons

The first wave of B6-D, from the concepts four open sets name. [`b6d1-1.png`](./b6d1-1.png) to [`b6d1-3.png`](./b6d1-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Families keep their base.** Each variant draws the body of its family: an open marker stays inside it, a closed one is a corner badge with the body cut around it (spec section 2.5). The bell takes its marker at the top right, as its spec exception says.
- **`heart-half` and `shield-half`** fill the half that is the meaning, like `star-half`; both are listed in `PARTIAL_FILLS` with their reason and a test.
- **`link-off`** carries the alias `unlink`; `layout-right` mirrors `sidebar` with the divider on the right; `sidebar` carries the alias `layout-left`.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `file-chart`, `file-cloud`, `file-edit`, `file-upload`, `heart-half`, `wifi-lock` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`battery-empty`, `link-off`, `map-pin-plus` and `shield-minus` are flagged for review.

## Measures

The cut is the smallest distance between two parts that do not touch, on the line drawing at 240 px; the ink is at 24 px; d is the nearest drawing of Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor and Heroicons.
