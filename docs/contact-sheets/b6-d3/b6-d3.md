# B6-D3: shapes, games, symbols and safety

The third wave of B6-D, from the concepts four open sets name. [`b6d3-1.png`](./b6d3-1.png) to [`b6d3-3.png`](./b6d3-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **`droplet-off`** is drawn for the concept `blur-off`, which is its alias.
- **Aliases on drawings that exist** carry the concepts the sets name: `grid` takes `grid-3x3`, `bicycle` takes `bike`, `fire` takes `flame`, and the CHANGELOG lists each pair.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `alien` and `shadow` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`bowling-ball`, `clover`, `code-square`, `first-aid-kit`, `gender-female`, `gender-male`, `graduation-cap`, `peace`, `radioactive`, `tennis-ball` and `text-wrap` are flagged and accepted (spec section 3, item 5): `code-square` is a family base plus a primitive, `gender-female`, `gender-male`, `peace` and `radioactive` are universal symbols, and the others keep the reading approved in their audit.
