# B5b: people and security

The second sub-batch of B5. [`b5b.png`](./b5b.png) shows the 11 line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred; [`b5b-filled.png`](./b5b-filled.png) shows the filled `shield-check` and, enlarged, `lock-open` and `id-card`. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **`user-minus`, `user-check`, `user-x`** draw the person of `user-plus` with their mark at the lower right. `user-x` blocks a user and `user-minus` removes one from a team; their labels say which.
- **`lock-open`** is the drawing of `lock` with its shackle raised and its left leg free.
- **`shield-off`** breaks the shield along its diagonal through the cut, as `eye-off` breaks the eye.
- **`id-card`** draws a bust whose shoulders are wide and flat: narrow ones under the round head read as a frowning face.
- **No 16 px master.** None of the 11 fuses at 16 px. The one fusion the measure reports, between the tower and the door of `building`, runs through the baseline both stand on, which joins them by design.

## Similarity

`shield` (d = 0.23) and `shield-check` (0.26) draw the shield's universal shape, accepted as a convention, as in B5a. Every other drawing has d > 0.30.
