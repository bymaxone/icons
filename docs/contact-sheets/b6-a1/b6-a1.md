# B6-A1: shapes, arrows and media

The first wave of B6, from the concepts the most open sets name. [`b6a1-1.png`](./b6a1-1.png) to [`b6a1-3.png`](./b6a1-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred; [`b6a1-redraws.png`](./b6a1-redraws.png) shows `star-half` and `film` beside `star` and `frame`. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Shapes** keep the set's corners: `square` is the squircle of `stop` at full size; `triangle`, `diamond`, `pentagon`, `hexagon` and `octagon` are regular polygons with rounded corners; `circle` is the ring of the `-circle` family.
- **`star-half`** is the outline of `star` with its left half filled, the first partial-state fill (spec section 2.5): the filled half is the rating. It is drawn at 96% of `star` to stay under the ink ceiling.
- **The `-circle` family** sets an arrow, play, pause or stop inside the ring of `alert-circle`, apart from it by the cut, as `plus-circle` does.
- **`divide`** draws its dots as rings of radius 0.75, three pixels across, since a 1.5 px dot is lost at 24.
- **`forward`** is the curved arrow of a message sent on, rising to the right; `redo` turns back on itself.
- **`film`** is a strip in portrait with short dashes as perforations down both bands, in the language of `frame`, which lies in landscape.
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.

## Similarity

Most of the batch is primitives and conventions (spec section 3, item 5): the geometric shapes, the diagonal arrows, the glyphs `asterisk`, `infinity`, `divide` and `copyright`, and the glyphs in a ring of the `-circle` family. `shuffle` (d = 0.22) and `disc` (0.24) are accepted as conventions. `star-half`, `forward`, `radio` and `film` are clear.

## Measures

The cut is the smallest distance between two parts that do not touch, on the line drawing at 240 px; the ink is at 24 px, under the 21.5% ceiling; d is the nearest drawing of Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor and Heroicons.
