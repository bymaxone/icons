# B6-A2: devices, files and calendar

The second wave of B6. [`b6a2-1.png`](./b6a2-1.png) to [`b6a2-3.png`](./b6a2-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred; the third sheet shows `calendar` beside its new variants and `hourglass`, the drawing that was `queued`. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Families keep their base.** The battery variants use the body of `battery`, the file and folder variants those of `file` and `folder`, and `commit` and `merge` the nodes of `branch` and `pull-request`.
- **Calendar variants** draw the calendar 14 units wide, where `calendar` is 15, so that a marker fits within the ink ceiling (spec section 2.5).
- **`battery-charging`** passes its bolt through the outline, which opens around it with the cut.
- **`location-arrow`** is a heading arrow pointing north; `paper-plane` is the message sent.
- **`hourglass`** is the drawing named `queued` until now: an icon is named after what it draws, so it takes the name of the object and keeps `queued` as an alias. The rename is listed in [`docs/redraws/b6-a2-devices-files-calendar.md`](../../redraws/b6-a2-devices-files-calendar.md).
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.

## Similarity

None of the batch is flagged. `file-minus` (d = 0.29) is the file of the set with the minus sign, and keeps the family's crease at a cut of 0.71, as `file`, `file-audio` and `file-video` do; `commit` holds its cut at exactly 1.5. Both are accepted as conventions.

## Measures

The cut is the smallest distance between two parts that do not touch, on the line drawing at 240 px; the ink is at 24 px, under the 21.5% ceiling; d is the nearest drawing of Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor and Heroicons.
