# B6-B1: interface, files and editing

The first wave of B6-B, from the concepts six or seven open sets name. [`b6b1-1.png`](./b6b1-1.png) to [`b6b1-4.png`](./b6b1-4.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Families keep their base.** The file, folder, clipboard, bookmark, shield and phone variants draw the body of their family.
- **Corner badges.** A marker that is a closed shape and does not fit inside its body becomes a badge in the bottom-right corner, the body cut around it: `calendar-heart` and `folder-lock` (spec section 2.5).
- **`contrast`** fills one half of its inner disc, as `PARTIAL_FILLS` allows.
- **`files`** draws the file of the set in front, scaled to 0.8 with its crease kept at the family's cut, and the edge of a second sheet behind it.
- **`lightning-off`** cuts the bolt of `lightning` 1.5 px either side of the slash, as `wifi-off` does.
- **`backspace`** is the key pointed to the left with a short minus; **`webhook`** is a curved arrow from one node to another.
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.

## Similarity

`equal`, `signal`, `voicemail` and `shield-plus` are flagged and accepted: the first three are conventions (spec section 3, item 5), and `shield-plus` is the shield of the set with a plus. `contrast` is accepted as a convention too.
