# B6-C3: nature, transport, health and shapes

The third wave of B6-C, from the concepts five open sets name. [`b6c3-1.png`](./b6c3-1.png) and [`b6c3-2.png`](./b6c3-2.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **`motorbike`** carries the alias `moped`, and `signpost` the alias `directions`.
- **`tractor`** rests its small wheel on the chassis line, as its large wheel rests on the body: touching parts, not a near miss.
- **At 16 px.** Rendered at 16 px and 1x by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `snail` fuses the centre of its spiral with its shell (pair 1-2), which is on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with the auditor's reason. `tractor` was redrawn until it rendered clean. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`rectangle` and `sphere` are flagged and accepted as primitives (spec section 3, item 5): each is the shape the concept is, drawn on this grid and stroke.
