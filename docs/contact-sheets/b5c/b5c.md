# B5c: data, time and files

The third sub-batch of B5. [`b5c-1.png`](./b5c-1.png), [`b5c-2.png`](./b5c-2.png) and [`b5c-3.png`](./b5c-3.png) show the 24 line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **`chart-bar`** draws its bars as single strokes on an L-shaped axis, apart from it by the cut; outlined bars beside each other read as `library`'s books.
- **`list`** sets bullets before its lines, which `menu` does not have; **`calendar`** marks its days with dots, and no digit.
- **`file-text`, `file-zip` and `file-plus`** draw the page of `file`, with its fold; the zip has two teeth and a pull, since a single line over a tab reads as "!". The fold sits 0.71 from the outline and fuses with it at 16 px, as in `file` itself.
- **`alarm-clock` and `stopwatch`** draw the face of `clock`.
- **`trending-up`, `trending-down`** keep their arrowhead apart from the line by the cut, and **`book-open`** keeps its two pages apart at the spine: both left the range of the usual drawings that way.
- **No 16 px master.** The fusions the measure reports at 16 px, in `calendar` and `table`, join parts through a third that touches both by design.

## Similarity

`list` (d = 0.22), `server` (0.17), `alarm-clock` (0.29) and `file-plus` (0.28) are accepted as conventions or as composites of the set's own drawings (`clock`, `file`). Every other drawing has d > 0.30.

## Measures

The cut is the smallest distance between two parts that do not touch, on the line drawing at 240 px; the ink is at 24 px, under the 21.5% ceiling; d is the nearest drawing of Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor and Heroicons.
