# B6-E1: arrows, interface and editing

The first wave of B6-E, from the concepts three open sets name. [`b6e1-1.png`](./b6e1-1.png) to [`b6e1-3.png`](./b6e1-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Aliases on drawings that exist** carry the concepts the sets name, and the CHANGELOG lists each pair.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `file-diff` and `file-star` fuse their crease with the outline, as `file` does at the family's cut, and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`chevrons-down`, `chevrons-left`, `chevrons-right`, `chevrons-up`, `exclamation`, `radio-button`, `registered`, `step-out` and `x-octagon` are flagged and accepted (spec section 3, item 5): the four `chevrons-*` are the set's chevron doubled, `exclamation` is a universal symbol, and `radio-button`, `registered`, `step-out` and `x-octagon` are interface or typographic conventions.
