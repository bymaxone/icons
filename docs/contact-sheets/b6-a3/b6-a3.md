## Language

- **`hammer`** has a solid striking face on one side of the head and a curved claw on the other; `repair` is the wrench.
- **`magnet`** is a horseshoe turned 45 degrees, its geometry rotated in the source rather than by a transform.

- **`hospital`** is a building on the ground with a cross and a door, and **`medal`** hangs from a ribbon, apart from `award`, a rosette.
