# R1, group 2: version, plan, style, hand, eraser, repair

These drawings read as something else at 24 px on the dark page, or carried no concept. Each item was drawn again in two or three variants, shown beside the drawing it replaces in [`group-2-variants-a.png`](./group-2-variants-a.png) and [`group-2-variants-b.png`](./group-2-variants-b.png), and the delegated auditor chose one. `version` took two more rounds, in [`group-2-version.png`](./group-2-version.png). [`group-2.png`](./group-2.png) shows the chosen drawings.

## What each drawing fixes

| Icon      | Was read as       | Now                                                                                                             |
| --------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `version` | a jar, a drum     | a stack of three cards: the two behind show only their edges, apart by the cut, and a dot marks the current one |
| `plan`    | a burger          | steps, a plan's levels                                                                                          |
| `style`   | a picture         | a paint roller                                                                                                  |
| `hand`    | a trident         | an open palm, fingers slightly apart, with a thumb                                                              |
| `eraser`  | two broken pieces | one wedge with its band, over a baseline                                                                        |
| `repair`  | a spoon, a key    | a wrench with an open jaw, in one outline                                                                       |

## Rejected on the way

- **`version`.** Nodes in a row measure d = 0.12 and 0.27 against Phosphor's `dots-three`; diamonds leading to a ring read as a key lying down, and standing up as a pendant; a document with a circular arrow reads as the action of restoring rather than the object.
- **`plan`.** A podium of three separate bars reads as a bar chart, which B5 draws as `chart-bar`; stacked chevrons would collide with `chevrons-up-down`.
- **`style`.** A fan of swatches is cluttered at 24 px.

## Measures
