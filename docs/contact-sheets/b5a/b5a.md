# B5a: actions, status and controls

The first sub-batch of B5, the general-purpose icons the first 148 drawings, drawn around the Studio, did not cover. [`b5a-1.png`](./b5a-1.png), [`b5a-2.png`](./b5a-2.png) and [`b5a-3.png`](./b5a-3.png) show the 25 line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred; [`b5a-filled.png`](./b5a-filled.png) shows the six filled drawings beside their line drawings. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **The circle family** (`plus-circle`, `minus-circle`, `close-circle`, `circle-slash`, `target`) uses the ring of `alert-circle`, r 8.5. `circle-slash` keeps its bar apart from the ring by the cut, where the usual "ban" sign touches it.
- **`check-square`** opens its square at a corner for the check to leave through, as `check-circle` opens its circle; filled, the square is solid and the check knocked out.
- **`file-import` and `file-export`** draw the page of `file`, with its folded corner, and the arrow crosses the page's edge through the cut. Without the fold they read as `log-in` and `log-out`.
- **`toggle`** is off in its line drawing, the knob on the left, and on in its filled one, the pill solid and the knob knocked out as a disc on the right.
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px, so none needs one.

## Similarity

A drawing within d = 0.30 of a third-party drawing is accepted only with a reason. `minus`, `arrow-up` and `arrow-down` are primitives. The others under 0.30 are universal conventions, where the right drawing is the same in every set, as with `alert-circle` in B1: a primitive in a ring (`plus-circle`, `minus-circle`, `close-circle`, `circle-slash`), two primitive chevrons (`chevrons-up-down`), `heart`, the IEC power symbol (`power`), a pill and its knob (`toggle`) and concentric rings (`target`). `bookmark` and `star` were redrawn to leave that range: a rounded top and a shallower notch (d from 0.19 to 0.46), sharper points (from 0.18 to 0.33).
