# B6-D2: symbols, home, games and places

The second wave of B6-D, from the concepts four open sets name. [`b6d2-1.png`](./b6d2-1.png) to [`b6d2-3.png`](./b6d2-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Typography and math** (`braces`, `brackets`, `regex`, `hd`, `hdr`, `pi`, `plus-minus`, `angle`, `diameter`, `radius`, `currency-bitcoin`) are conventions (spec section 3, item 5).
- **`traffic-light`** carries the alias `traffic`; `diamond` carries `diamond-suit`; `hotel` is the wide building with an H.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `cable-car`, `contract`, `hdr` and `sailboat` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`braces`, `brackets`, `diameter`, `hdr`, `radius` and `record` are flagged for review.
