# B6-F7 and B6-F8: objects and the last variants

The last two waves of the line set: twenty devices, tools, finance and education objects, and eight more icons (one musical instrument, one office object and six variants retried with a smaller marker). [`b6f78-1.png`](./b6f78-1.png) to [`b6f78-3.png`](./b6f78-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Each new object was rendered next to the existing icons of its category** before it was drawn into the sheet, so a drawing that repeated one was dropped.
- **`card-lock`, `card-refund`, `mail-star`, `mail-warning`, `server-bolt` and `file-warning`** are variants whose first attempt broke the ink limit; their markers are drawn smaller than the usual one.
- **`currency-lira`** draws the Turkish lira sign, a typographic convention like the other currencies.
- **`paint-roller`** is the former `style`, renamed for the object it draws (`docs/redraws/f78-objects.md`).
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `file-warning` fuses its crease with the outline, as `file` does at the family's cut, and is on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

`smart-lock` is flagged for review.
