# B6-B2: objects, science and health

The second wave of B6-B, from the concepts six or seven open sets name. [`b6b2-1.png`](./b6b2-1.png) to [`b6b2-3.png`](./b6b2-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Objects in profile or front view.** Each object is drawn in the view that names it: `flask`, `beaker` and `test-tube` from the front, `telescope` and `flashlight` from the side.
- **`weight`** is a kettlebell on a flat base, and **`recycle`** three arrows around a triangle, cut at each corner.
- **`tools`** draws the wrench of `repair` in front of a screwdriver, cut 1.5 px around it.
- **`certificate`** carries its seal as a corner badge (spec section 2.5).
- **No 16 px master.** None fuses at 16 px.

## Similarity

No drawing is flagged.
