# B6-T4: more family variants

The fourth top-up of icon families. [`b6t4-1.png`](./b6t4-1.png) to [`b6t4-3.png`](./b6t4-3.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Each variant keeps the convention its family has on main**, as in B6-F5: an open marker sits inside the body of a family that keeps its markers there, a closed marker is a corner badge with the body cut around it, and the bell takes its marker at the top right.
- **`video-x` and `video-check`** follow `video-plus`: the marker sits at the lower right of the body, which is cut around it, and the lens stays whole.
- **`cart-dollar`** is labelled Cart total, so it does not compete with `currency-dollar` in the search; `file-pen` is an alias of `file-edit`.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `file-info`, `wallet-check` and `wallet-x` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

No drawing of the batch is flagged.
