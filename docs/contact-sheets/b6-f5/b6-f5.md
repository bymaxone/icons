# B6-F5: family variants

The fifth wave of the category fill, the variants of icon families that had few or none. [`b6f5-1.png`](./b6f5-1.png) to [`b6f5-4.png`](./b6f5-4.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Each variant keeps the convention its family has on main.** An open marker sits inside the body of a family that keeps its markers there, such as `file`, and a closed marker is a badge in the bottom-right corner with the body cut around it (spec section 2.5). The bell takes its marker at the top right.
- **The cog is not a corner marker.** No `-cog` variant is drawn and `settings` covers configuration.
- **`mail-invite`** is renamed `mail-plus`, with `mail-invite` as a deprecated alias (`docs/redraws/f5-variants.md`). `list-details` is an alias of `list`.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `file-clock`, `file-question`, `file-share`, `file-user`, `database-lock`, `image-search`, `server-lock` and `wallet-plus` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).

## Similarity

No drawing of the batch is flagged.
