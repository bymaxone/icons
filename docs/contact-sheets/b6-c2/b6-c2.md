# B6-C2: household, places, objects and games

The second wave of B6-C, from the concepts five open sets name. [`b6c2-1.png`](./b6c2-1.png) and [`b6c2-2.png`](./b6c2-2.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Appliances share one body.** `fridge`, `microwave` and `washing-machine` draw a rounded cabinet with one detail that names it: the handle, the window with its buttons, the drum.
- **`satellite-dish`** draws the concept `satellite`, which is its alias; `door` carries the alias `door-closed`, `playing-cards` the alias `cards`, `solar-panel` the alias `solar-power` and `chess-knight` the alias `strategy`.
- **At 16 px.** Rendered at 16 px and 1x by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `fridge` fuses its handle with its outline (pair 0-2) and `podcast` fuses its two broadcast arcs (pair 1-2); both are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with the auditor's reason. `microwave` and `washing-machine` were redrawn until they rendered clean. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).
