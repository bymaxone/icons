# B6-F6 and B6-T5: transport, places and family variants

The sixth wave of the category fill (eleven vehicles and buildings) and the fifth top-up of family variants (forty-two). [`b6f6at5-1.png`](./b6f6at5-1.png) to [`b6f6at5-4.png`](./b6f6at5-4.png) show the line drawings at 48 and at 24 and 16 px 1x on the dark and light pages, and blurred. The delegated auditor approved them on behalf of the founder.

## Language

- **Variants follow the convention of their family on main**, as in B6-F5: an open marker sits inside the body of a family that keeps its markers there, a closed marker is a corner badge with the body cut around it, and a `-off` drawing carries the slash of the family.
- **`egg-off`, `fish-off`, `milk-off` and `beer-off`** form one family that marks a food free of an ingredient.
- **`file-play`** is an alias of `file-video` and `list-check` of `list-checks`, which draw the same thing.
- **At 16 px.** Tested by `pnpm check:sixteen`, `copy-check`, `copy-plus`, `pharmacy` and `webhook-off` fuse a pair and are on `scripts/sixteen-allowlist.json` with a reason. None of the batch has a 16 px master (spec section 4.2).
