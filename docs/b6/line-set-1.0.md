# The line set of 1.0

The line set closes with the drawings that passed the bar of every batch: an original drawing on the 24 grid that reads at 24 px, is not another icon under another name, stays inside the ink and cut limits, and was approved by the delegated auditor on behalf of the founder. The count is what the bar allowed, not a target: the founder's decision of 2026-11-13 is that 1.0 ships at the same quality bar and the set keeps growing in 1.x.

## Counts

Measured on the files of `icons/` when the set closed:

| Folder         | Files |
| -------------- | ----- |
| `icons/line`   | 1,311 |
| `icons/filled` | 51    |
| `icons/sm`     | 45    |
| `icons/brands` | 14    |

Every candidate was matched against the file names of fourteen reference sets (names only, never drawings) and drawn only when at least three of them carry the same meaning.

## What was left out, and why

The auditor's decisions, grouped by the reason. A name here was drawn or considered and did not pass; none is a promise.

- **No reading at 24 px.** Anatomy (`stomach`, `liver`, `kidney`, `intestine`, `bone-broken`, `x-ray`), most animals beyond the ones that read (`hamster`, `gorilla`, `raccoon`, `zebra`, `whale`, `orca`, `dolphin`, `kangaroo`, `flamingo`, `parrot`, `squirrel`, `llama`, `donkey`, `unicorn`, `peacock`, `pelican`, `woodpecker`), bowl foods (`rice`, `salad`, `soup`, `cereal`, `spaghetti`), most buildings (`tunnel`, `ferry`, `igloo`, `observatory`, `hangar`, `prison`, `stadium`), most instruments (`guitar`, `violin`, `harp`, `maracas`, `tambourine`) and tools such as `drill`, `pliers` and `multimeter`, and variants whose marker turned into a blob (`folder-share`, `image-edit`, `map-pin-x`, `lock-check`, `bookmark-edit`).
- **Another icon under another name.** `courthouse` is `bank`, `barn` is `garage`, `windmill` is `wind-power`, `smart-home` is `home-signal`, `barbell` is E3's `barbell`, `charger` is `plug`, `fax` is `printer`, `stylus` and `crayon` are `pencil`, `file-pen` is `file-edit` and `image-off` without its parts is a crossed-out frame. Only `file-pen` became an alias, of `file-edit`; the other names are not drawn.
- **Ink or cut.** The drawing broke the ink limit or the cut between parts and could not be simplified without losing its reading: `cinema`, `school-bus`, `calendar-share`, `camera-rotate`, `truck-return` and `truck-loading`.
- **A letter or a symbol reading.** `tuning-fork` reads as a Y, `dart` as the male sign, `walker` as an A, `maracas` as 99, `roundabout` as a crosshair.
- **Trademark and risk.** Silhouettes at risk of a registered shape (`sloth`, `walrus`, `otter`, `mole`, `lizard`), the `nfc` mark, and `hand-point`, whose single raised finger reads as an obscene gesture.
- **The cog as a marker.** No `-cog` variant is drawn: rendered at 16 px the cog fills 47% of its badge box, against 17 to 36% for the other markers (spec section 2.5).
- **Out of 1.0 by decision.** `zodiac-*` and `hurricane`.

## After 1.0

These are not rejected. They wait for 1.x, one attempt each with a new idea:

- **Categories:** buildings beyond the few that read at 24 px, clothing, celebration, toys, music, and the balanced set of beliefs and places of worship (`church`, `mosque`, `synagogue`, `temple`, `yin-yang`).
- **Deferred drawings, with the idea to try:** `passport` (the top half of a globe as an arc with a meridian, or an open booklet with a stamp), `football` (a ball with three curved panels), `baseball` (one stitched seam), `engine`, `axe` (a symmetric labrys), `repeat-once`, `popcorn` (kernels as separate rings), `smog`, `avalanche`, `earthquake` (cracked ground) and `sun-moon`.
